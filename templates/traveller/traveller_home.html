About Us

Welcome to OpenRoutes

At OpenRoutes, we believe that travel should be accessible to everyone, regardless of physical ability. Our mission is to break down barriers in tourism by connecting travellers impacting by disabilities with specialised tour operators who understand their unique needs.

We meticulously verify accommodations, transport, and itineraries to ensure they meet high accessibility standards. Whether you need wheelchair-friendly paths, sensory-sensitive environments, or specialized assistance, OpenRoutes opens the world to you.

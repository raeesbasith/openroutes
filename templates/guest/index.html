About Us

Welcome to OpenRoutes

OpenRoutes is dedicated to making travel accessible for everyone. We believe that physical or cognitive challenges should not limit your ability to explore the world. Our platform connects travellers with specialized tour operators who understand and cater to diverse accessibility needs.

Whether you are looking for wheelchair-friendly destinations, sensory-friendly tours, or assistance with travel logistics, OpenRoutes is here to help you plan your perfect trip with confidence and ease.

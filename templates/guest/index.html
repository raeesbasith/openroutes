About Us

Welcome to OpenRoutes

OpenRoutes is dedicated to making travel accessible for everyone. We believe that physical or cognitive challenges should not limit your ability to explore the world. Our platform connects travellers with specialized tour operators who understand and cater to diverse accessibility needs.

Whether you are looking for wheelchair-friendly destinations, sensory-friendly tours, or assistance with travel logistics, OpenRoutes is here to help you plan your perfect trip with confidence and ease.

Services

Our Accessible Services

Accessible Transport

We provide vehicles equipped with wheelchair lifts, ramps, and secure locking systems to ensure safe and comfortable travel for everyone.

Verified Accommodation

All our partner hotels and resorts are physically verified for accessibility features like ramps, wide doors, and adapted bathrooms.

Specialized Guides

Our tour guides are trained in sign language, first aid, and disability etiquette to ensure a supportive and informative experience.

Equipment Rental

Don't worry about bringing everything. We rent out wheelchairs, mobility scooters, and sensory kits at your destination.

Custom Itineraries

We design pacing and routes specifically for your needs, avoiding steep terrains and ensuring frequent rest stops.

Caregiver Support

Professional caregivers can be arranged to accompany you during the trip for medical or personal assistance.

Sensory-Friendly Tours

We offer quiet hours, tactile experiences, and descriptive tours for travellers with autism or visual impairments.

24/7 Support

Our dedicated support team is available round-the-clock to handle any accessibility-related emergencies or requests.
